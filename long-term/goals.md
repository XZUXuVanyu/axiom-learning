# Long-term goals and milestones

Updated: 2026-09-07.

## Learning goals

1. Advanced C++ and practical project design through working features; basic/intermediate TypeScript deferred.
2. Harness plugins and bridges, eventually DAW/CAD integration.
3. Support ongoing development without taking over crucial implementation.

Background: C/C++ basics, OOP, some data structures, STM32 firmware experience; limited TypeScript. These are reported background, not course assessments.

## Practical outcomes

1. One reliable standalone C++ capability with a clear request/result/failure contract.
2. One reusable plugin callable from a fresh Codex session, including invalid-request handling and a second use case.
3. An offline physical-audio renderer with stated assumptions, units, reproducible output, and independent reference checks.
4. A possible bubble/water-sound instrument and later VST prototype. These are directions, not a validated physical model or a committed delivery date.
5. Incremental Axiom evidence, approval, application-bridge, and instrument workflows, driven by demonstrated needs.

Use the alternating four-study-week trial in [weekly-plan.md](weekly-plan.md). A damped oscillator is the proposed first physics exercise; it does not by itself validate bubble acoustics. Do not wait for the entire Axiom platform before investigating a bounded model.

## Acceptance and delegation

Measure progress by explainable, tested, reusable behavior, not commit count or elapsed time. Earlier time estimates were provisional and lacked reliable study-hour measurements; no delivery deadline is established.

The learner owns crucial learning implementation. Administrative records and review are assistant work. Broader production delegation remains a future decision: define a component's contract, assumptions, independent checks, failure modes, and acceptance owner, and deliberately record any dated rule revision before delegating crucial code.

Product usefulness and differentiation are hypotheses. Prefer one useful complete path before generalizing infrastructure.
